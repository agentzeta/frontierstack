The premise

The decisive systems will cross technical boundaries.

Agents will spend money. Robots will act in public space. Biology will become programmable. Capital markets will run continuously. Quantum risk will reach infrastructure built decades earlier. Compute will shape national power. The summit studies these frontiers together because their consequences will arrive together.

The six fields

Research · Products · Policy · Capital

01

Agentic AI

Agents that act, transact and coordinate

Identity, mandates, memory, evaluation, payments and recovery for autonomous systems.

02

AGI and ASI

Human agency at the capability frontier

Control, ownership, scientific acceleration, economic transition and institutional design.

03

Quantum and cyber security

Infrastructure that survives the next threat model

Cryptographic migration, quantum resistance, verifiable execution and operational recovery.

04

Longevity and biosciences

Engineering longer, healthier lives

Biomarkers, programmable medicine, clinical infrastructure and responsible access.

05

Onchain capital markets

Rights, money and markets as software

Tokenisation, stablecoins, digital securities, agentic finance and enforceable claims.

06

Robotics, chips and compute

The physical stack of intelligence

Embodied systems, semiconductors, energy, inference economics and sovereign capacity.

Summit format

Built for consequential exchange

Short provocations establish a disputed claim. Working sessions test it against technical constraints and institutional consequences. The programme closes with concrete research questions, venture opportunities and policy decisions.
